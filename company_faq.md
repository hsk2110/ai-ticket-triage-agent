# Acme SaaS Company Knowledge Base & Support Policies

## Password & Account Recovery
Users can reset their password at any time by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
If the automated reset email does not arrive within 5 minutes, users should check their spam folder. 
For security reasons, support agents cannot manually issue passwords over email; users must verify their identity via SMS authentication.

## Billing & Invoices
Invoices for subscriptions are automatically sent on the 1st of every month to the primary billing email address.
Users can download past invoices as PDFs by navigating to Settings -> Billing -> Invoice History.
Refunds are provided within a 14-day window from purchase upon written request.

## Database & System Outages
If a production database experiences an error or 500 status code, our engineering team is automatically notified via PagerDuty.
Customers should check status.acme-saas.com for live incident updates.
Support representatives should immediately tag any production down report as HIGH priority and escalate to On-Call.

## Feature Requests
Feature requests are reviewed weekly by our Product Management team.
Users can vote on upcoming features at feedback.acme-saas.com.
Support agents should thank the user for the feedback and provide the direct link to the feedback portal.
